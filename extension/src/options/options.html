Instant sync (recommended)

Makes your browsers share bookmark changes within a second or two, with no folder picking. Setup takes about 30 seconds, once.

  1. Click Set up instant sync.
  2. Open the downloaded file SyncMark-Setup. If your browser warns about it, choose Keep.
  3. Come back here. It connects by itself.
